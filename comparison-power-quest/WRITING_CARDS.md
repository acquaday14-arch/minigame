# Typed picture cards

Current game: Comparison Power Quest. Full mode contains the unchanged 30 grammar activities plus two typing cards; picture-only mode contains only the two cards. Each writing card awards one point when all three checks pass. Feedback is a transparent rule-based check for the explicitly requested simple sentences, not an unrestricted AI essay score or speech/pronunciation assessment. Learners can revise and recheck before saving; editing invalidates the previous check. Saved answers lock and are counted once.

Assets: `assets/speaking-heights.webp` and `assets/speaking-bags.webp`, generated with the built-in image tool and optimised locally. Exact names/heights/prices are rendered in HTML rather than baked into the illustration.

Height data: Leo (red) 140 cm; Mia (blue) 150 cm; Ben (green) 160 cm. Compare exactly two names with taller/shorter than. Correct contractions, common degree modifiers and accurate differences in centimetres are supported.

Price data: red £20; green £35; blue £50. Use the cheapest/the most expensive with a named bag/backpack/one. Forward and inverted sentence orders, contractions and optional group phrases are supported. Capitalisation and final punctuation do not affect these card checks. Sentences outside the stated scope are asked to use the supported simple form rather than being claimed to be universally ungrammatical.

Checks: requested complete sentence pattern; target comparison phrase; consistency with the given facts. Percentages are 0/33/67/100 based on these checks, not a calibrated language-proficiency score. Draft checks do not award points or submit records. Teacher Records uses the saved final score and the active total (2 or 32).

## Original image prompts

### Card 1

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: speaking practice picture for an English classroom web game about comparatives. One wide landscape illustration showing exactly THREE school-age children standing upright next to each other on the SAME flat baseline, feet all visible, plain pale lavender classroom wall and floor background. Left: short boy wearing a RED T-shirt. Middle: a girl wearing a BLUE T-shirt, visibly taller than left boy. Right: boy wearing a GREEN T-shirt, visibly taller than both other children. The height differences must be clearly visible and consistent, all three at same distance from camera, front-facing, no perspective tricks, no extra people. Friendly polished 2D workbook cartoon, rounded shapes, clear outlines, bright soft colours, warm smiles, simple outfits. Each child occupies a separate equal-width third of the picture with generous space between. No text, names, letters, height numbers, rulers, charts or watermark; exact names and measurements will be displayed in HTML below the picture. Sharp and clear enough for a classroom TV.

### Card 2

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: speaking practice picture for an English classroom web game about superlatives and prices. One wide landscape illustration showing exactly THREE fully visible school backpacks displayed side by side on the same shop shelf, all facing forward, same apparent size and at the same distance from camera. Left backpack RED, middle backpack GREEN, right backpack BLUE. No other bags or products. Simple pale lavender shop wall background, gentle shadows. Friendly polished 2D workbook cartoon with rounded shapes, clear outlines and bright soft colours, consistent with a children's classroom comparison card. Each bag occupies a separate equal-width third with generous space. No text, logos, prices, numbers, labels, price tags or watermark; exact prices will be displayed in HTML below the picture. Sharp and uncluttered for a classroom TV.
