The Boy Who Learned Kind Words

Read the story as text

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Ethan. Ethan was a good boy, but he often forgot to use kind words. One day, his teacher, Mrs. Harper, gave him a challenge. She said, “Try using kind words every day for a whole week.”

Ethan decided to try his best. He said “please” when he asked for help. He said “thank you” when someone helped him. He said “sorry” when he made a mistake. He even said “you did a great job” to his friend!

At the end of the week, Ethan felt happy. His friends were happy too. Everyone smiled more and the class felt like a team. Ethan learned that kind words can make the world a better place.
