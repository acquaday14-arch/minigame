# Original lesson assets

Built-in image generation was used for all illustrations. WebP files in `assets/` are locally stored, optimised crops of these original illustrations. Park panels are `park-a.webp` and `park-b.webp`; the six four-picture sets are `odd-1-1.webp` through `odd-6-4.webp`.

The park was visually checked: A has a taller tree, a red ball, two ducks and a girl reading. B has a shorter tree, a blue ball, three ducks and a girl drinking. Other incidental artistic details are not scored.

Lesson format reference: https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/young-learners/paper/movers/format/

This is original practice, not an official exam. Speaking Part 1 practises describing differences. Speaking Part 3 practises selecting a different picture and explaining why. The game uses explicit category checks for automatic scoring; other well-reasoned oral answers may be discussed with a teacher. Speech is not recorded or automatically graded.

## Generation prompts

### Illustration 1

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: original illustration for a children's A1 Movers spot-the-differences web game. Create ONE wide image with TWO equal side-by-side panels separated by a clear white gutter. No words, numbers or labels anywhere. Friendly crisp 2D storybook cartoon, thick clean outlines, bright soft colours, large obvious objects, minimal detail. Both panels show the SAME simple park: one tree at far left, one wooden bench at right with a girl sitting on it, one large ball on grass in front, and a small pond at bottom with ducks. Keep locations, girl clothes, bench, pond, sky and camera identical between panels. Exactly FOUR intended differences: (1) LEFT tree very tall, RIGHT tree much shorter; (2) LEFT ball red, RIGHT ball blue, equal ball size; (3) LEFT pond exactly TWO separate yellow ducks, RIGHT pond exactly THREE separate yellow ducks; (4) LEFT girl holding and reading an open book, RIGHT same girl holding and drinking from a cup, no book. Girl is large enough for action to be clear. Fully show all objects, no cropped tree, no extra animals, no people except the one girl in each panel. Landscape composition, sharp and suitable for sharing on a classroom TV.

### Illustration 2

Use case: scientific-educational. Create ONE classroom picture-card sheet as a precise grid of 3 rows and 4 columns, twelve equally sized rectangular cells. Crisp friendly 2D cartoon illustration, thick outlines, bright soft colours, plain very light blue backgrounds, thin grey straight grid lines, generous white space, no words, no numbers, no letters, no watermark. Each cell contains exactly ONE large centred fully visible subject, no extra props, no overlapping grid boundaries. Row 1 left to right: a red apple; one yellow banana; an orange fruit; a wooden chair. Row 2 left to right: a red bus; a blue bicycle; a green car; a grey elephant. Row 3 left to right: a pink T-shirt; one blue sock; a green hat; a slice of chocolate cake on a tiny plain plate. Match the same children's workbook cartoon style across every cell. Make the objects instantly recognisable to A1 children. Landscape sheet, every cell same dimensions, objects fill most of each cell. This is an image asset to crop into individual cards, so keep all objects strictly inside their own cells.
