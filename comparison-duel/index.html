How to play

Take turns on one screen. Each player gets 8 grammar questions and 8 unique speaking cards. Cards never repeat within a match.

Grammar: +10 for a correct answer. Speaking: up to +15, awarded by the teacher or your partner using three checks. No microphone is needed.

After each speaking card, open one mystery box: +5, +10, +15, +20, +35, −5, −10 or −15. Each player has the same set, shuffled separately. Scores may go below zero. Highest total wins; equal totals are a draw.

Learning points and correct answers are recorded separately from luck. No timer—take time to speak.
