📖 Quick grammar review

Present Simple: habits, general truths and timetables.
I/you/we/they + base verb; he/she/it + -s/-es.
Negatives: don’t / doesn’t + base verb.
Signals: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day.

Present Continuous: now, around now and temporary situations.
am / is / are + verb-ing.
Signals: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, look, listen.

Examples: I play football every Saturday. / I am playing football now.
She likes ice cream. / She is eating ice cream.
They go to school by bus. / They are going to school now.
